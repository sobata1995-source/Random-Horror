# v0.9.0 — Halloween Edition (Beta)

- Replaced dungeon and lobby lanterns with glowing jack-o'-lanterns at the same positions.
- Includes four scenarios in a shuffled cycle, 12 maze layouts and randomized diary-page locations.
- Includes Easy, Normal and Nightmare, four original music stages and randomized scare scenes.
- Shows the gold-block escape instruction after the third objective, with a three-second head start.
- Prevents repeated prisoner respawning when its entity handle is unavailable.

Singleplayer beta for Minecraft Bedrock. Tested during development on Minecraft for Windows 26.52. Use a new dedicated world and activate both packs. Cheats can remain OFF. Minimum engine version is 1.21.90. Mobile, consoles and Realms are unverified.
